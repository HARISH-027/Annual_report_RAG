Identify risks that the reports themselves disclose or that the figures clearly evidence.
Group under: Financial (liquidity, credit, forex, interest), Operational, Regulatory/Legal, Market/Commodity, ESG/Safety, Concentration.
For each risk: one-line description, supporting numbers or quote with citation, and the mitigation the company states (if any).
Do not add industry risks that the evidence does not mention. Rank by materiality when evidence allows.
